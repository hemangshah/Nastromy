## Getting Help

If you have a question, found a bug, or want to suggest a feature, please email us at **nastromyapp@gmail.com**.

When reporting a bug, it helps to include:

- What you were doing when the issue occurred
- Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
- Your Nastromy app version (Settings → About in the app)

You can also tap **Contact Us** inside the app (Settings → About → Contact Us) and the relevant device information will be pre-filled automatically.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is today's APOD not showing?

NASA publishes a new APOD each day (Monday–Friday). If today's photo hasn't appeared yet, pull down on the list to refresh. NASA occasionally delays or skips a day — this is on NASA's side and outside our control.

### The app shows older photos, not today's

Pull down on the list to refresh. If the issue persists, check your internet connection.

### How do I clear cached images?

Go to **Settings → Clear Image Cache**. This removes downloaded images from your device. They will be re-downloaded the next time you view them.

### How do I remove all my favorites?

Go to **Settings → Favorites → Remove All Favorites**. This cannot be undone.

### How do I turn off daily notifications?

Go to **Settings → Daily APOD Reminder** and toggle it off. You can also manage notification permissions in your iPhone's Settings app under Nastromy.

### Can I use APOD images?

All images in the app are sourced from NASA. Most NASA content is in the public domain, but some photos are copyrighted by the credited photographer. Always check the copyright shown on the image before reusing it, especially for commercial purposes.
